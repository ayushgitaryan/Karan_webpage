Karan Aujla

Karan Aujla, whose real name is Jaskaran Singh Aujla, is an Indian Punjabi singer, rapper and songwriter.

He is famous for his unique voice, powerful lyrics and modern Punjabi music style.

Some of his popular songs include Softly, Winning Speech, Tauba Tauba and Wavy.

He has established himself as one of the popular names in Punjabi music.
